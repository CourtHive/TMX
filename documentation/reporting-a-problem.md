# Reporting a problem in TMX

When bug reporting is available for your provider, use **Help → Report a problem**. Unexpected-error
notifications and the Invalid Participants dialog may also offer **Report a problem**. Routine warning
messages do not offer it. Sign in with your provider account before sending.

## Describe what happened

Enter what went wrong. You can also describe the result you expected and the steps that led to the
problem. The form includes the current screen and tournament context. It does not save, undo or replay
your tournament work.

Leave participant names, dates of birth, contact details and other personal information out of the
text. Reports include limited technical error categories and validation counts, but no tournament
snapshot, screenshots, raw console log or participant details.

Select **Send report**. A successful receipt gives an opaque reference and the report's expiry date.
Keep that reference if you need to discuss the problem with support. **Check receipt** refreshes its
status without filing another report.

If reporting is temporarily unavailable, the form says the report was not confirmed and keeps your
text. Keep the form open and retry later. Do not treat an unconfirmed submission as received; drafts
are not stored after you close the form or reload TMX. If your account or provider changes, reopen the
form under the correct account before sending.

## Receive the resolution email

Support will send the investigation outcome and any fix or workaround to your verified account
contact address. You can file a report before verifying that address; email waits for verification.

From the report form, select **Open account settings**. Review or enter your account contact email,
save it and follow the verification link sent to that mailbox. You can resend the existing verification
email from the same screen. This changes the contact address on your account for all reports; it is
not a separate address for this report.

An email marked **accepted** has been accepted by the email service. Delivery is still unconfirmed;
TMX does not claim it reached your inbox. Support investigates and writes the response manually, so
there is no automatic fix or promised response time.

## Retention and Time Warp

Reports and their report-specific details expire **30 days after submission**, including open reports.
Support moves investigations that need longer into its ticketing system with the necessary technical
information.

Reporting is unavailable during Time Warp and while returning to your tournament. If Warp starts
while a report form is open, your text remains visible but sending and account changes are disabled.
Close it and open a fresh report after returning. Errors from the disposable Warp copy are not added
to the production report's technical evidence.
